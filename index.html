RHA Collection

Rice husk ash is sourced directly from rice mills in Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry, ensuring traceable and consistent raw material quality.

Learn More

Pre-Processing

Physical cleaning and controlled thermal treatment are used to remove carbon residues and preserve amorphous silica structure.

Learn More

Chemical Purification

Acid leaching and multi-stage washing remove metallic impurities, enabling high-purity silica recovery.

Calcination & Milling
