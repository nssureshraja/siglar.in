Frequently asked  questions

What purity level does SIGLAR target?

SIGLAR targets consistent 99% silica (SiO₂) purity through controlled processing and multi-stage purification.

What industries can use SIGLAR silica?

SIGLAR silica is suitable for tyre, rubber, ceramics, fillers, composites, and other industrial applications requiring consistent high-purity SiO₂.

How is Rice Husk Ash converted into silica?

Through controlled burning, acid leaching, washing, calcination, and grinding processes designed to remove impurities and preserve amorphous silica structure.

What purity level does SIGLAR deliver?

SIGLAR targets and validates 99% silica (SiO₂) purity with batch-wise quality checks and documentation.

Is SIGLAR focused on sustainable materials?

Yes. SIGLAR converts agricultural waste into valuable industrial materials, reducing environmental impact and supporting circular manufacturing.

Where is SIGLAR currently operating?

SIGLAR operates in Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry, leveraging local rice mill clusters and MSME technical facilities.

SIGLAR is focused on transforming Rice Husk Ash into high-purity silica through sustainable, scalable, and industry-aligned processes.
